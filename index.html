Creator workflow

Clip. Edit. Ship.

Clip n Ship is a desktop application for discovering video moments, editing clips, organizing local media, and preparing content for publishing on supported social platforms.

Our goal is to give creators a practical workflow from video discovery to final upload, with control over their own content and connected accounts.

In development · Not publicly distributed

How the application works

Creators can work with video files locally, review content in a media library, and choose where to publish. Some integrations require authorization from the relevant social platform and may not yet be available.
